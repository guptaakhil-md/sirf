Why

Stay connected. Skip the spiral.

Instagram is where your friends are. It's also built to keep you there long after you've finished talking to them. Sirf keeps the first part and removes the second.

Open for a reason, leave when you're done

You come in to read a message or reply to one. When that's done there's nothing left to look at, so you put the phone down. No feed waiting underneath.

Nothing to scroll

No home feed, no Reels tab, no Explore, no suggested posts. The screens that turn "two minutes" into an hour aren't hidden behind a setting. They're not there.

Your people, not the algorithm

What you see is what someone chose to send you. A reel from a friend opens and plays, and when it ends you're back in the chat, not in a feed of a hundred more.

What changes

Same Instagram. Most of it removed.

Sirf is Instagram's own messages, exactly as they are on instagram.com, shown in an app that refuses to go anywhere else.

Install

Three taps on any Android phone.

Sirf isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks one extra question the first time. That's all.

Download the file

Tap the button at the top of this page. Your phone saves a small file called Sirf-release.apk.

Open it and allow

Android will say your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, switch on "Allow from this source", go back, tap Install.

Log in once

Open Sirf and sign in like you would on instagram.com. You stay logged in from then on.

When a new version is required, Sirf tells you inside the app and gives you a download button. Nothing ever installs by itself.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is my account safe?

Sirf is a window onto instagram.com. It doesn't log in for you, doesn't read your messages, and never talks to Instagram's servers on its own. It only hides parts of a page you're already logged into, the same category of thing as an ad blocker.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's a small independent app, shared directly from this page. Direct installs are how Android has always let you run software you chose yourself.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. iPhones in India can only install apps from the App Store, and Apple doesn't allow apps like this one there.

Can I still see a reel a friend sent?

Yes. It opens and plays. What you can't do is swipe on to the next one. When it's over, you're back in the chat.
